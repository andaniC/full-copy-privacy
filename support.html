Full Copy help

Copy the pictures, not just the words.

Using Full Copy

  • Copy with pictures: select text and images on any page and press ⌘C (Ctrl+C on Windows). Paste into Google Docs, Notion, Mail or Word and the pictures come along.
  • Paste into AI chats: in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Slack, Discord and WhatsApp Web, ⌘V attaches every picture and marks in the text where each one was. On any other site, right-click the text box and choose Paste with images.
  • Collect from several pages: press ⌥⇧C (Alt+Shift+C) on a selection, or right-click a picture and choose Add to collection. One paste drops them all in.
  • Your copies: click the Full Copy icon in Chrome’s toolbar to copy any of your last 60 copies again.

Full Copy Pro

  • The free trial. Full Copy Pro starts with 7 free days: click the Full Copy icon and choose Start free trial. Your card is needed to start, nothing is charged until the 7 days are up, then it’s $5 a month. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. Without Pro, Full Copy steps aside and your saved copies wait for you.
  • Switching Pro on. After you pay, click Switch on Full Copy Pro on the thank-you screen and Pro switches on by itself. On another computer, open your receipt email there and click the same button. You can also paste the license key from that email into Full Copy’s Pro page, under Enter a license key instead.
  • One key, three browsers. To move Pro to another browser, open the Pro page in one you no longer use and choose Remove from this browser. Lost that browser? Email us and we’ll free its place.
  • Cancel any time with Manage subscription on Full Copy’s Pro page or in your receipt email. Pro stays on until the end of the month you paid for.
